About VoltedLife

VoltedLife is an independent app studio. We design, build and publish mobile apps for iPhone and other devices, with a focus on personal reflection, everyday convenience and privacy.

How we build

One clear purpose per app.

Each app does one job and does it well.

Privacy by default.

We collect only what an app needs to work. We have no ads, no third-party analytics and no tracking, and we never sell personal data.

Your data is yours.

Where an app offers an account, you can delete it and all of its data from inside the app.
