Common questions

Connect the Dots: Do I need an account?
No. Connect the Dots works fully without an account, and your journeys stay on your device. Sign in only if you want to sync across devices.

Connect the Dots: How do I delete my account and data?
In the app, go to Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all synced journeys, moments, photos and videos from our servers. You can also email privacy@voltedlife.com.

Quiet Salah: Does it share my location?
No. Your location is checked on your phone against the app's built-in mosque list. Quiet Salah has no server and does not send your location to us or anyone else.

Quiet Salah: Why doesn't my iPhone go silent by itself?
iOS doesn't let apps change Focus directly. Follow the in-app iPhone setup to add the Quiet Salah Shortcuts once. After that, arrival and departure can switch Do Not Disturb for you.

Quiet Salah: A mosque is missing or in the wrong place.
Email support@voltedlife.com with the mosque's name and address. Our mosque data comes from OpenStreetMap and Overture Maps. You can also improve OpenStreetMap directly at openstreetmap.org.
