Connect the Dots

Your life's moments, connected.

Coming soon for iPhone and iPad

Connect the Dots is a private, reflective timeline of your life story. You create a "journey" and add the moments that defined it: a title, a date (as exact as a day or as rough as a year), your age at the time, a few words of reflection, and photos or videos. For each moment you choose how it felt on a scale from struggle to joy. Connect the Dots then places your moments as dots and draws the path that connects them, so you can look back and see how the hard times and the good times fit together.

  • Works fully without an account. Everything stays on your device.
  • Optional sign-in (Sign in with Apple) to sync your journeys privately across your devices.
  • Print or save a journey as a PDF.
  • No ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking.
  • Delete your account and all synced data at any time from inside the app.

Quiet Salah

Silence at the mosque, automatically.

Coming soon

Quiet Salah silences your phone when you arrive at a mosque and lets it ring again when you leave, so you don't have to remember. It ships with a built-in directory of mosque locations in the United States, and your phone uses that list to recognize when you arrive.

  • On Android: it turns on Do Not Disturb when you arrive and restores your previous setting when you leave (with your permission).
  • On iPhone: iOS doesn't let apps change Focus directly, so Quiet Salah sends an arrival notification and works with a Shortcut you set up once to turn Do Not Disturb on and off.
  • Location is checked on your phone against the built-in mosque list. Quiet Salah does not send your location to us or to anyone else.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Mosque data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0), and Overture Maps Foundation Places data (see docs.overturemaps.org/attribution).
